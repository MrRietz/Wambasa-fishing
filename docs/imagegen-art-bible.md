# Wambasa Fishing Imagegen Art Bible

## Purpose

Use this as the initial consistency guide when replacing Phaser-drawn placeholders with generated bitmap graphics.

## Shared Style

- Nordic coastal-industrial mood
- 2D painted game art, readable at browser-game scale
- Slightly muted natural colors with warm brass and worn metal accents
- Mist, rain, cold morning light, wet wood, brushed metal, and workshop surfaces
- No licensed logos, ABU marks, exact product likenesses, or brand trade dress

## Camera And Scale

- Factory: fixed isometric or three-quarter top-down workshop view
- Fishing: top-down or elevated three-quarter boat and water view
- Equipment: centered product-style illustrations with transparent or easily removable flat backgrounds
- Fish: side-view catch illustrations, readable silhouettes, consistent scale

## Prompt Template

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: browser game bitmap asset
Primary request: <specific asset>
Scene/backdrop: Swedish coastal-industrial world, Svängsta workshop or Wambasa inshore water

Lighting/mood: cold morning light, light mist, restrained melancholy
Color palette: muted sea green, weathered wood, dark iron, brass highlights, off-white labels
Materials/textures: wet timber, brushed metal, enamel paint, worn rubber, cloudy water
Constraints: no logos, no readable brand names, no exact real product replicas, no watermark
```
